abstract: >
  We analyse the structure of optimal dynamic withdrawal policies in guarantee products that promise
  a minimum lifetime income. The policyholder controls the withdrawal rate at each period, subject
  to contractual caps and the depletion boundary of the personal account. By formulating the
  problem as a singular stochastic control problem and characterising the value function through
  a system of variational inequalities, we identify the qualitative features of the optimal
  policy — including the withdrawal timing, the boundary between full and partial withdrawal
  regimes, and sensitivity to mortality and interest rate parameters. Numerical experiments
  illustrate the economic significance of dynamic versus static withdrawal strategies.

summary: >
