abstract: >
  We establish weak convergence results for a class of path-dependent stochastic differential equations
  that arise in the pricing of basket credit default swaps with simultaneous counterparty risk and
  contagion risk. The contagion mechanism is modelled through a jump-diffusion framework with
  interacting intensities. We prove functional weak convergence of the associated empirical measures
  and derive pricing functionals that are continuous with respect to the weak topology, enabling
  numerical approximation via simulation of the limiting system. The theoretical framework applies
  to a broader class of path-dependent functionals encountered in multi-name credit derivatives.
