abstract: >
  Robo-advisory platforms rebalance client portfolios subject to regulatory suitability constraints,
  transaction cost thresholds, and lot-size restrictions that require integer share quantities.
  The resulting rebalancing problem is a mixed-integer optimisation that is computationally
  intractable at the scale of thousands of concurrent accounts. We formulate drift minimization
  under lot-size constraints as a practical integer-share rebalancing problem and analyse its
  structure, propose efficient heuristics with provable approximation guarantees, and evaluate
  these on institutional rebalancing scenarios typical of retail robo-advisory deployments.
