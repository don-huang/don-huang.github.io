abstract: >
  Portfolios in institutional wealth management are increasingly constructed from heterogeneous
  product wrappers — funds, structured notes, insurance-linked products — each with distinct
  fee, liquidity, and risk-factor structures. Standard single-layer attribution frameworks
  conflate product-level and asset-class-level effects, obscuring the true drivers of performance.
  This paper proposes a two-layer attribution methodology that separates allocation effects at the
  product-wrapper level from those at the underlying asset level, enabling cleaner decomposition
  of active returns in mixed-product portfolios.
