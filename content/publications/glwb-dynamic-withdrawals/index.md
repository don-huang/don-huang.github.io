abstract: >
  We study the optimal initiation strategy for a Guaranteed Lifelong Withdrawal Benefit (GLWB) contract
  under a framework that allows dynamic (variable) withdrawal rates. The policyholder's problem is
  formulated as a combined optimal stopping and stochastic control problem. We derive the associated
  Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman variational inequalities and develop efficient numerical schemes, with
  particular attention to the structure of the free boundary that separates the waiting region from
  the initiation region. Numerical results illustrate how contract parameters, mortality rates, and
  market conditions jointly determine the optimal initiation threshold.
