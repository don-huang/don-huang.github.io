abstract: >
  We develop regression-based Monte Carlo algorithms for solving stochastic control problems that
  arise in the valuation and risk management of variable annuity products with lifelong withdrawal
  guarantees. The key challenge is the high-dimensional nature of the state space when policy features
  such as ratchet provisions and surrender options are incorporated. We adapt the Longstaff–Schwartz
  least-squares Monte Carlo approach to the control setting, establish convergence properties of
  the regression estimator, and demonstrate the method's accuracy and computational efficiency
  relative to finite-difference benchmarks across a range of contract specifications.
