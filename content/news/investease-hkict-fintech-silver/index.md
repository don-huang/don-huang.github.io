AQUMON's InvestEase solution received a FinTech Silver Award in the Banking, Investment & Insurance category of the Hong Kong ICT Awards.

InvestEase applies AI-driven portfolio construction and digital advisory technology to institutional wealth-management workflows. The award recognizes its combination of technological innovation and practical application in financial services.
