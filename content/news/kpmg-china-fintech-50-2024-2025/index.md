AQUMON was recognized in KPMG China's FinTech 50 programmes in both 2024 and 2025. The recognition highlights the company's work in applying artificial intelligence to wealth management, including the InvestEase platform.

The programmes bring together financial institutions, technology companies, and industry participants to examine emerging developments in China's fintech sector. AQUMON's inclusion reflects its continued focus on translating quantitative research and AI methods into deployable investment technology.
