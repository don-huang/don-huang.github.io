Presented at the HKUST MSc in Financial Mathematics Orientation, introducing the MAFS6010H Quantitative Investment Systems course (Winter 2026-27). The session covered the complete investment decision chain — information, model, forecast, portfolio, trade, and monitoring — and highlighted key topics including factor investing, portfolio optimization, direct indexing, financial machine learning, LLM-driven signals, and optimal execution.
