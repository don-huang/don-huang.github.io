The AI-Driven Automatic Wealth Management R&D project was completed in March 2024. Supported by Hong Kong's Innovation and Technology Fund, the three-year project developed a modular architecture spanning client profiling, goal-based portfolio construction, monitoring, rebalancing, and execution infrastructure.

The work combined financial mathematics, machine learning, and production engineering to support scalable and auditable digital advisory workflows. It also established a reusable research-validation-deployment foundation for institutional implementations.
