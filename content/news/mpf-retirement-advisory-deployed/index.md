AQUMON's AI-driven MPF retirement advisory system has been deployed with institutional partners in Hong Kong, including integrations with AIA and BCT Group.

The system combines personalized retirement-gap analysis, lifecycle and asset-liability-management-informed optimization, and explainable fund recommendations. Its architecture is designed for regulated pension and insurance environments, connecting quantitative retirement research with practical advisory workflows.
